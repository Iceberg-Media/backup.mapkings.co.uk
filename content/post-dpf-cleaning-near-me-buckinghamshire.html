If your DPF warning light is on, your car has lost power, or you're seeing poor fuel economy, your diesel particulate filter might be blocked. Here's what to do about it.

What Is a DPF?

Your Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) sits in the exhaust system and captures soot particles from diesel combustion. It's designed to self-clean through a process called regeneration — burning off the soot at high temperatures during longer drives.

Signs Your DPF Is Blocked

  • DPF warning light on the dashboard
  • Loss of power or limp mode
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Strong diesel smell
  • Failed MOT on emissions
  • Engine management light on

Why DPFs Get Blocked

Short trips and city driving are the biggest cause. If you only do short journeys, the exhaust never gets hot enough for regeneration to complete. The soot builds up until the filter is clogged.

Prevention Tips

  • Take your car on a 30+ minute motorway drive at least once a week
  • Don't ignore the DPF warning light
  • Use the correct engine oil (low-ash spec)
