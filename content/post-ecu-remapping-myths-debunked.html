There's a lot of misinformation about ECU remapping. Let's bust the most common myths.

Myth 1: Remapping Damages Your Engine

Reality: A professional remap stays within safe operating parameters. We don't push engines beyond their design limits — we optimise within them. The factory tune is deliberately conservative to meet emissions regulations across all markets. A proper tune unlocks what the engine can safely do.

Myth 2: You'll Fail Your MOT

Reality: A standard remap will pass MOT. The emissions stay within legal limits. What fails MOT is removing the DPF, catalytic converter, or AdBlue system — those are separate modifications.

Myth 3: Remapping Isn't Reversible

Reality: We always save your original ECU file. If you want to go back to stock, we can flash it back anytime. Takes about 30 minutes.

Myth 4: It Voids Your Warranty Completely

Reality: It voids the powertrain/engine warranty specifically. Other warranties (body, electrical, interior) are unaffected. Once your warranty expires, there's no downside.

Myth 5: All Remaps Are the Same

Reality: Huge difference between a custom tune and a generic file. A custom tune is read from YOUR car's ECU, adjusted for YOUR engine's condition, and verified with diagnostic data. A generic file could be from any car.

Myth 6: You Need New Exhaust/Intake for a Remap

Reality: For Stage 1, you need zero modifications. The stock hardware is fine. Exhaust and intake upgrades are for Stage 2 and above.

Myth 7: Remapping Kills Your Fuel Economy

Reality: The opposite is often true. A well-tuned engine operates more efficiently. Many customers report better MPG on motorway runs after a remap, especially diesel engines.

Myth 8: Diesel Cars Can't Be Remapped

Reality: Diesels are actually the BEST candidates for remapping. They have the most to gain because factory tunes are heavily detuned for emissions compliance.

Myth 9: You'll Notice the Difference Immediately

Reality: Actually, this one is TRUE. Most customers feel the difference within the first few miles. More responsive throttle, stronger pull through the rev range, and smoother power delivery.

Myth 10: Remapping Is Only for Boy Racers

Reality: Our most common customers are everyday drivers who want their car to feel better. More torque for overtaking, smoother acceleration, better fuel economy. It's about making your daily drive more enjoyable.
