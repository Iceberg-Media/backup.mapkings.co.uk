What Affects the Price?

Several factors influence what you'll pay for an ECU remap:

  • Your vehicle make and model — Some ECUs are easier to read and write than others. Popular cars like BMW, Audi, and VW are typically straightforward.
  • The type of remap — A basic Stage 1 is cheaper than a Stage 2 or Stage 3 which requires supporting hardware.
  • Whether it's mobile or workshop-based — Mobile services like ours save you the trip. We come to your home or workplace anywhere in Buckinghamshire.
  • The tuner's experience — Cheap remaps from unknown tuners can cause engine damage. Always choose a reputable specialist.

Is a Cheap Remap Worth It?

You'll see ECU remaps advertised online for as little as £50-£100. These are usually generic file downloads — not custom-tuned to your specific engine. A proper remap involves reading your ECU, adjusting the calibration, and writing it back with settings tailored to your car. Generic files can cause:

  • Excessive exhaust gas temperatures
  • Increased fuel consumption
  • Transmission damage on automatics
  • MOT failures
