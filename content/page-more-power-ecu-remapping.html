More Power from ECU Remapping

Your engine is capable of much more than the factory allows. Manufacturers deliberately detune engines to meet emissions regulations across all markets, account for poor fuel quality, and ensure reliability in extreme conditions. A professional ECU remap unlocks the power that's already there.

How Much More Power?

A Stage 1 remap typically adds 20-40% more horsepower to your engine. For a BMW 330i with 252hp stock, that's around 310hp after the tune. For a VW Golf GTI with 245hp, you're looking at approximately 310hp.

Where Does the Extra Power Come From?

The ECU (Engine Control Unit) controls fuel injection, ignition timing, boost pressure, and throttle response. Factory settings are conservative — a professional remap optimizes these parameters within safe limits to extract maximum performance.

Is It Safe?

Yes. A professional remap stays within the engine's design limits. We don't push beyond what the hardware can handle — we optimize within safe parameters. Every tune includes diagnostic checks before and after.
