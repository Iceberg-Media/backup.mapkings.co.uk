Pops & Bangs Tuning

Add excitement to your drive with pops, bangs, and crackle tuning. Professional ECU calibration that gives your exhaust a sporty, aggressive sound on deceleration.

What Are Pops & Bangs?

Pops and bangs (also called crackle maps or exhaust burbles) are calibrated into the ECU to create a sporty exhaust sound when you lift off the throttle. Unburnt fuel ignites in the exhaust, creating the signature crackle.

Is It Safe?

When done professionally, pops and bangs tuning is safe for your engine and exhaust. We calibrate within safe limits to avoid damage to catalytic converters or exhaust components. Always remove catalytic converters before running aggressive pops and bangs maps.

What Cars Can Get It?

Most turbocharged petrol engines can benefit from pops and bangs tuning. Popular cars include VW Golf GTI/R, BMW M cars, Audi S/RS models, and Ford Fiesta/Focus ST.
