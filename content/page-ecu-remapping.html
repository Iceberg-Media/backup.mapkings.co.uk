ECU Remapping Services in Buckinghamshire

ECU remapping is the process of modifying your engine's control unit software to optimize performance. We offer Stage 1, 2, and 3 remaps depending on your car's setup and goals.

Our Equipment

We use professional-grade tools for all our remapping work:

  • Alientech — Industry-leading ECU tuning and programming tools
  • Magic Motorsport Flex — Advanced ECU, TCU, and gearbox tuning solution

Every remap file is custom-made for your specific vehicle — never generic, never off-the-shelf. We read your ECU's original data, calibrate it for your car's condition and your performance goals, then test and verify the results.

Stage 1 — Software Only (£180)

A software-only tune on a completely standard engine. No hardware changes needed. Typically adds 20-40% more power and torque. Perfect for daily drivers.

Stage 2 — Bolt-On Mods (£200)

For cars with supporting hardware — downpipe, intercooler, intake, or decat. Bigger gains of 40-60% more power.

Stage 3 — Built Engine (POA)

Full-built performance spec with hybrid turbos, upgraded fuelling, and forged internals. Price on application.
