Mobile ECU remapping means we bring the workshop to you. No need to drive to a garage, leave your car all day, or arrange a lift home. Here's how it works.

How Mobile Remapping Works

  1. You book an appointment — choose a date, time, and location (home, workplace, anywhere)
  2. We arrive with our equipment — laptop, diagnostic tools, and ECU tuning hardware
  3. Diagnostic check — we scan your car for any existing faults before we start
  4. Read the ECU — we connect to your car's OBD port and read the current software
  5. Apply the tune — we upload the custom calibration to your ECU
  6. Test and verify — we run diagnostics again and do a test drive to confirm everything is perfect

How Long Does It Take?

  • Stage 1 remap: 1-2 hours
  • Stage 2 remap: 1.5-2.5 hours
  • DPF/EGR solutions: 1-3 hours depending on the service

What Do You Need From Me?

Just your car and the keys. We bring everything else. Ideally, the car should be parked somewhere with enough space for us to work safely. A driveway, car park, or quiet street is perfect.
